# LittleLog: Baby Care

A simple log for baby care and mother's medicine, including milk, diapers, daily care, medicine doses, and pumping.

## How to use

Choose a tab—Milk, Poop/Pee, Daily Care, or Mother—and add an entry using the date and time picker. Tap a saved entry to edit it, or swipe left to delete it. All entries appear in History, where you can search and filter by Baby or Mother.

## FAQ

### Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored locally on your device in app-protected storage. Nothing leaves your phone.

### Do I need an account?

No. LittleLog does not require an account.

### Does my data sync between devices?

No. Your data stays on the device where you entered it.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove all its data.

### What iOS version do I need?

iOS 26 or later.

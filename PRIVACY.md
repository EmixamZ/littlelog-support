# Privacy policy

**LittleLog: Baby Care**  
**Last updated: 2026**

LittleLog stores all data only on your device using iOS-protected storage; the app has no server or cloud service. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking of any kind (including for children), or embedded third-party libraries or SDKs. Deleting the app removes its stored data. Device backups are controlled by your iOS backup settings. For privacy questions, contact [your email].
